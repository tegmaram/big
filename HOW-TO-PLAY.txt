Jamal Craft 26.3 - how to play
================================

OFFLINE (the single HTML file)
  Put JamalCraft-26.3.html on local storage (not a cloud/network drive).
  Open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. First boot takes 20-60 s.
  Phones: turn the phone sideways. Give the tab 3-4 GB of free RAM.

LAN / WEB (the split build)
  Do NOT double-click index.html - its game files are Brotli sidecars and need
  a real HTTP server.
  Windows: double-click start-server.bat
  macOS:   double-click start-server.command (or ./start-server.sh)
  Linux:   ./start-server.sh
  Then open the URL it prints, on this PC or on any phone/PC on the same WiFi.
  Type the PIN once per device if one is shown.

PHONES
  Landscape is required. The Jamal Craft touch layer gives you a joystick,
  jump/sneak buttons, inventory/chat/pause chips and a tap-able hotbar.
  If the touch layout is wrong for your device, add ?touch=native to the URL
  (or ?touch=bedrock to force the custom layer). iPhone: Share -> Add to Home
  Screen for fullscreen without the URL bar.

PRIVATE

Worlds are stored in the browser. Use Export World before clearing site data.
